Arlo Studio

A personal video-production application for the YouTube channel Arlo Again and its owner's other YouTube channels.

What the app does

  • Helps the channel owner plan, script, voice and render original animated videos on his own computer and phone.
  • Uploads the finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API (youtube.upload), sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails and the publishing time.
  • Reads public statistics of the owner's own channels (views, likes, comments) to plan better videos.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner and his own Google/YouTube accounts. The app is not offered to the public and does not access other people's channels or data.

Google user data

The app uses Google sign-in only to upload and manage videos on the signed-in user's own channel. Data is stored locally on the owner's devices, is never sold or shared, and can be removed at any time. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Arlo Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
