Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Arlo Studio ("the app") is a personal application used by the owner of the YouTube channel Arlo Again to produce and publish videos on his own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data we access

  • Google account / YouTube data — when you sign in with Google, the app requests permission to upload and manage videos on your YouTube channel (YouTube Data API scopes such as youtube.upload and youtube.readonly). It reads your channel name, ID and the list and statistics of your own videos.
  • OAuth tokens — access and refresh tokens are stored locally on your device so the app can upload scheduled videos.
  • Content you create — scripts, voice files, images and videos made in the app stay on your devices.

How we use the data

Only to provide the app's features: uploading your videos, setting their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish time, and showing your channel's statistics to you. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data is transferred only to Google/YouTube when you upload or manage your own videos.

Limited Use

Arlo Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and deletion

All data is stored locally on your own computer and phone. You can delete it at any time by signing out in the app settings and deleting the app folder. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; we will delete any stored tokens on request within 7 days.

Security

Tokens are kept only on your devices and are sent only to Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change accordingly.
